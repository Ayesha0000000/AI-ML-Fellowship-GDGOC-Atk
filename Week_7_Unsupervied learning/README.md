Week 7 - Unsupervised Learning and Market Basket Analysis
Introduction

In this week, we studied Unsupervised Learning and worked on a project called Market Basket Analysis. The goal of this analysis is to find patterns and relationships between items that customers buy together.

Learning Objectives

The main objectives of this week were to understand unsupervised learning techniques, discover hidden patterns in data, and analyze customer behavior.

Topics Covered

Introduction to Unsupervised Learning
We learned about unsupervised learning where the model works with unlabeled data and finds patterns on its own.

Market Basket Analysis
We studied how to analyze shopping data to find which items are frequently purchased together.

Association Rules
We learned how association rules help in identifying relationships between items.

Support, Confidence, and Lift
We understood important metrics used in market basket analysis:

Support shows how often items appear together
Confidence shows the likelihood of items being bought together
Lift shows the strength of the relationship between items

Pattern Discovery
We discovered useful patterns that can help businesses improve sales and recommendations.

Practice Work

In this week, we applied market basket analysis on a dataset and generated association rules to identify item relationships.

Conclusion

This week helped us understand how to analyze data without labels and discover hidden patterns. We learned how businesses use this technique for recommendations and decision-making.
