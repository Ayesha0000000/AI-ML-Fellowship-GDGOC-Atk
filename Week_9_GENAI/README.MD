**Duration:** 1 Week  
**Method:** Use → Improve → Understand → Build  
**Developer:** Ayesha

## About This Sprint

This sprint introduces Generative AI through hands-on implementation. The goal is to move from using existing tools to building your own solutions. It covers two major areas — Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and Large Language Models (LLMs) with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

---

## Topics

### GAN Fundamentals

GANs consist of two neural networks — a Generator and a Discriminator — that compete against each other. The Generator tries to create fake data, while the Discriminator tries to detect it. Over time, the Generator gets better at producing realistic outputs.

- **Simple GAN Implementation** — Build a basic GAN from scratch to understand the training loop, loss functions, and how the two networks interact.
- **GANs for Image Generation** — Train a GAN on image datasets to generate new, realistic images that never existed before.
- **Data Augmentation using GANs** — Use GANs to generate additional training data, which helps improve model performance when real data is limited.

### LLMs & Retrieval

Large Language Models are powerful AI models trained on massive text datasets. They can understand and generate human-like text. RAG improves LLMs by letting them retrieve relevant information from a knowledge base before generating a response.

- **Working with LLMs** — Learn how to load, prompt, and interact with large language models effectively.
- **RAG-based Applications** — Build a pipeline that retrieves relevant documents and uses an LLM to generate accurate, context-aware answers.
